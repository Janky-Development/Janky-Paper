## Paper - the server Janky Paper is built on

Janky Paper is a fork of **[Paper](https://papermc.io)** ([source](https://github.com/PaperMC/Paper)), made by
the **PaperMC team** and **[Paper's contributors](https://github.com/PaperMC/Paper/graphs/contributors)**.
Almost all of the server is their work: the patched game server, the plugin API, the chunk system, and years of
performance and stability fixes. Janky Paper only adds its own changes on top. Their code stays under its authors'
copyright and Paper's license (GPL-3.0; some contributors also chose MIT, listed in Paper's `LICENSE.md`).

If Janky Paper is useful to you, Paper is what makes it work. Please consider supporting them:
https://papermc.io

Paper builds on:

- **Spigot** and **CraftBukkit** - SpigotMC and their contributors
- **Bukkit** - the Bukkit project and its contributors
- **[paperweight](https://github.com/PaperMC/paperweight)** - PaperMC's build tooling, which Janky Paper uses too

And none of it would exist without **Minecraft** by Mojang Studios.

contributor, and **Paper** (the PaperMC team and contributors) as the project Janky Paper is built on. That
includes keeping this file. This is an additional term of the license (GNU GPL v3, section 7(b));
[NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) lists exactly what has to stay.
