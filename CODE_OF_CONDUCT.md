# Code of Conduct

Janky Paper is a small project run in people's free time. Everyone taking part (issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews) is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to server software or Git
- criticise code and ideas, not people
- assume good intent, and ask before assuming
- keep discussions on topic and in good faith

Not accepted:

- harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks
- discrimination of any kind
- publishing someone's private information
- spam, trolling, or repeatedly derailing discussions
- pressure or threats over bugs, features or response times

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who don't follow this,
temporarily or permanently, depending on what happened.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through the contact options on the
[repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Pepe-tf). Reports are kept confidential.
